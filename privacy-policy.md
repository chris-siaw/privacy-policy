# Privacy Policy — World Capital Cities Trivia

**Last Updated:** 5th June 2026

---

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how World Capital Cities Trivia ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the App. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### a) Automatically Collected Data
We collect anonymized analytics data via Amplitude, including:
- Device type and model (smartphone, tablet)
- Operating system version
- App version
- General geographic region and language preferences
- Gameplay interactions and session data

### b) Device Identifier
A randomly generated device identifier (UUID) is created and stored securely on your device. This identifier is used to associate your leaderboard progress with your device and does not identify you personally.

### c) Leaderboard Data
When you play, we store the following on our servers (Supabase):
- Your chosen display name
- Your device identifier
- Game statistics: total points, questions answered, correct answers, and accuracy rate

This data is visible to other users on the in-app leaderboard.

### d) Sign in with Apple (Optional)
If you choose to sign in with Apple, we receive and store your Apple User ID (an anonymized identifier provided by Apple) to link your leaderboard progress to your Apple ID. This allows your scores to persist across devices. We do not receive your real name or email address unless you explicitly choose to share them with us via Apple's consent flow, and we do not store them.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data to:
- Display your scores and ranking on the in-app leaderboard
- Preserve your progress across sessions and, if signed in with Apple, across devices
- Analyze usage patterns to improve gameplay and app performance
- Diagnose and fix technical issues

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

### Amplitude
We use Amplitude for anonymized analytics. Amplitude processes usage data under its own privacy policy. No personally identifiable information is shared with Amplitude.
Website: [amplitude.com](https://amplitude.com/privacy)

### Supabase
We use Supabase to store leaderboard data (display names, game statistics, device identifiers, and Apple User IDs for signed-in users). Data is stored on Supabase-managed infrastructure. Supabase processes data under its own privacy policy.
Website: [supabase.com](https://supabase.com/privacy)

### Apple Sign in with Apple
Sign in with Apple is provided by Apple Inc. When you use this feature, Apple's privacy policy applies to the authentication process. We only receive the anonymized Apple User ID from Apple.
Website: [apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy)

---

## 4. Public Leaderboard

Your chosen display name and game statistics (points, questions answered, accuracy) are displayed publicly on the in-app leaderboard. All users of the App can see this information. You can change your display name at any time in the Settings screen.

---

## 5. Data Retention

Leaderboard data is retained for as long as you use the App or until you request deletion. Amplitude analytics data is retained per Amplitude's standard retention policy.

---

## 6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical safeguards to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
